Guides for long-distance couples

Updated 3 November 2026

Plain, practical help for being together when you live apart. How to spend an evening, what to write, how to fit two clocks into one day, and which apps are worth a place on your phone.

We make Our Tiny Planet, an app for couples that is coming soon. These guides are written to be useful whether or not you ever use it.

The guides

  • Games to play in a long-distance relationshipQuestion games, drawing, word games, chess and ludo, cosy co-op and watch-together nights, with how to play each on a call.
  • Long-distance date night ideasCook the same recipe, film nights, virtual museums, stargazing at the same time and dates that arrive in the post.
  • Open when letters: ideas and how to write themPrompts for hard days, good news and missing each other, how to write each letter, and paper or digital.
  • How to make time zones work in a long-distance relationshipFind your overlap window, build rituals around it, send messages that can wait, and protect your sleep.
  • Best apps for long-distance couplesA fair roundup by job: calls, messages, watching together, calendars, games, couple apps and letters.
