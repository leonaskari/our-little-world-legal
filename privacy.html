Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Our Tiny Planet is an app for couples, whether together or long distance: a small shared world you build together, with a home, buildings, chat, letters, games and more. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have.

“Our Tiny Planet”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the provider of the app. You can contact us at hello@ourtinyplanet.com.

Who can use the app

Our Tiny Planet is for adults only. You must be 18 or over to create an account. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and do not let anyone younger continue.

What we collect

Your account

  • Sign-in details: your email address and a password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, never readable by us), or your Google or Apple account if you choose to sign in with one. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your real email address.
  • Profile: your first name, date of birth, time zone and app language, your avatar (the look you choose for yourself), any looks you and your partner design for each other, which only the two of you can see, and the other choices you make about your profile, such as the mood you set, your free-to-talk hours and the name you call your partner.
  • Settings: your notification choices and quiet hours, and the other choices you make in Settings.
  • Presence: whether you have the app open right now, and when you were last active. Only your partner sees this, and you can hide when you were last active in Settings.
  • Live updates: when something changes in your world (for example a new letter, or a move in a game you are playing or watching together), our servers pass it straight on to your partner's phone. Our database host keeps these passing updates for no more than 3 days.

What you and your partner create together

Our Tiny Planet is shared between you and one partner. When you pair up with an invite code, the two of you share a world. We store what you create in it so that both of you can see it:

  • your land, home designs, buildings and the things you place in your house;
  • chat messages, letters (including ones scheduled for later), notes and voice notes;
  • photos you add (for example to a photo frame, your journal or as your chat background);
  • journal entries (each one either shared with your partner or private to you), your calendar, countdowns and your vision board;
  • your answers to the question of the day (your partner sees yours once they have answered too);
  • games you play together, your shared pet, pulses and moods, movement check-ins, and the water and sleep you log in the Vitality Dome.

Anything you share in the app can be seen by your partner. Private journal entries can only be seen by you.

Your approximate location (only if you choose)

If you choose to share your sky, the Weather Window and its widget show your partner the weather where you are, and slow mail flies between your two towns. It only does this if you turn it on. We then store your location rounded to about 11 km (never your exact position), and only your partner can see it. Without it, slow mail uses the town of your time zone instead. You can turn this off at any time in the app.

Your steps from Apple Health or Health Connect (only if you choose)

Walk to each other in the Vitality Dome can read your daily step count from Apple Health (on iPhone) or Health Connect (on Android), but only if you allow it. We read nothing else from Health: no other health or fitness data, and we never write anything to it. We store one number for each day (your steps that day) so that your partner can see how far the two of you have walked. Your steps are never used for advertising or marketing, never shared with anyone other than your partner, and never sold. You can stop sharing at any time in your phone's Settings (Health, then Data Access) or in Health Connect, and you can still type your steps in yourself instead. Your steps are health data, so we only read them with your explicit consent: allowing it in the Health dialog is that consent, and you can withdraw it at any time. We keep your daily steps for as long as you have an account, so that the two of you can look back on how far you have walked; you can delete them whenever you like in Settings → Account → Delete some of your data.

Your moods, water and sleep (only if you choose)

You can share how you feel with a mood, and log your water and sleep in the Vitality Dome. None of this is required: you choose whether to use each one, and you can stop at any time. Because this is health-type data, we store it only with your explicit consent, which you give by using each one, and we use it for one thing: showing it to your partner. It is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone other than your partner, and never sold. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Account → Delete some of your data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your phone so that we can send you notifications (for example when your partner sends you a message). We do not use it for anything else.

Besides what your partner does, a few notifications come from the app itself: the question of the day, a gentle evening reminder when your partner has kept your streak going and you have not been in yet, a welcome back after several days away, and reminders about your pet. Each has its own switch in Settings → Notifications. If you have a free trial of the Couple Plan, we also tell you two days before it ends that it will renew unless you cancel; as it is about a payment, it has no separate switch, but it never comes if notifications are off.

Crash reports and usage (only if you choose)

Both are off unless you turn them on in Settings → Privacy.

  • Crash reports include your account ID, the app version, your type of phone’s operating system and details of the error, so that we can fix problems.
  • Usage events include only the name of what happened (for example “a building was opened”, or roughly how long a visit lasted, such as “1 to 5 minutes”), a random ID for your phone that is not linked to your account, and the app version and operating system.

Records the app keeps to run

Some features need simple records of what happens in your world: the dates you opened the app (for your streak, welcome back and awards), when each of your buildings reached a new level, how a game of pool went (shots, fouls and balls potted), and when the Couple Plan or the Comet packs were shown to you, started or bought. These are kept with your account, whatever your choice about usage events. We also count them up, across everyone, to see how the app is doing and to improve it (for example how many couples come back after a week, or how many free trials turn into subscriptions). Only we see them; they are never used for advertising, never shared and never sold. The dates you opened the app are kept for 1 year, the record of a welcome back for 90 days, and the rest for 13 months; all of them go sooner if your account does. The payment records from RevenueCat (see Purchases) are kept for our accounts, but after 13 months they are no longer linked to your account. You can object to this use, as explained under Your rights.

Purchases

You can buy Comets (an in-app currency), or a bundle that opens a building's upgrades for your couple, through Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Payment is handled entirely by them, with our payments service RevenueCat confirming each purchase. We receive a record of what you bought: the pack or bundle, a transaction reference, which store, and when. From RevenueCat we also receive, for each purchase, renewal, cancellation or refund, the price, its currency and the country of your store account. For the Couple Plan subscription, we also keep which plan your couple has, who subscribed, when it renews or ends, and whether it is a free trial, so both of you get it. We never see your card or bank details. If a purchase is refunded by the store, we record the refund and take back the Comets it gave (as many as are still in your wallet).

The waitlist on our website

Before the app is out, you can leave your email address on ourtinyplanet.com to hear when it is. We store your email address and when you signed up (we send you one welcome email to say you are on the list, with a way off it), and a scrambled form of your internet address that cannot be turned back into it, only to stop the form being misused. We use it for one thing: to email you once when Our Tiny Planet is out. We will not send you anything else unless you separately agree to it. To leave the list, use the unsubscribe link in any of our emails or write to hello@ourtinyplanet.com. We delete your address once the launch email has gone (within 30 days of it at most), or sooner if you ask.

Reports and feedback

If you send us feedback or report a problem (Settings → Legal and support → Report a problem), we store what you write with your account ID, the app version and your phone’s operating system, so that we can look into it and, for reports, keep the app safe. We read every report within a day.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show you adverts or use your information for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Why we use your information (and our legal basis)

  • To provide the app to you and your partner: your account, your shared world and everything in it. The legal basis is the contract between us (our Terms of Use).
  • To keep the app safe and working, including checking you are 18 or over and preventing misuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interests in running a safe service, and the contract.
  • The records the app keeps to run, counted up to see how the app is doing: our legitimate interests in understanding and improving the app. You can object to this at any time.
  • Your approximate location, notifications, crash reports, usage events and the website waitlist: only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the app or your phone’s settings.
  • Your steps from Apple Health or Health Connect, and the moods, water and sleep you choose to log: these are health-type data, so we use them only with your explicit consent. You give it by turning each one on, and you withdraw it by turning it off or deleting the data in the app. None of it is ever used for advertising or shared with anyone but your partner.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run the app. They only process your information on our behalf and to provide their service:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, file storage (for photos and voice notes), sign-in, live updates between your phones and backups, in London.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: deliver notifications to your phone.
  • Google and Apple: if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Open-Meteo: provides the weather for the Weather Window. It receives only the rounded location, not who you are.
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google: for purchases. RevenueCat receives your account ID and your purchase history so that it can confirm purchases.
  • Resend: sends our emails, including sign-in emails (such as a password reset) and the website waitlist emails. It receives your email address and the email we send you. Our emails show a small image from our own storage, so opening one tells our storage that the email was opened, as opening any web page would, but not who you are.
  • Cloudflare and Google (Gmail): Cloudflare runs our domain and passes emails sent to hello@ourtinyplanet.com on to a Gmail mailbox, where we read and answer them.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website. Like any web host, it sees the internet address of each visitor in its logs. The app itself never talks to it.
  • Wikidata and Wikipedia: the film details in Film night (titles, years, summaries) come from them. Our server fetches them; your phone never contacts them and they learn nothing about you.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your database, files and backups stay in the UK (and backups possibly in the EU). Some of the providers above, such as Google, Apple, RevenueCat, Resend, Cloudflare and GitHub, may process information in the United States or elsewhere outside the UK. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards data protection law requires: an adequacy decision (such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for companies certified under it) or the provider's data processing terms with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for details of these safeguards.

How we keep it safe

Information is encrypted when it travels between your phone and our servers. Access rules in our database make sure that only you, and your partner for what you share, can read your world. Photos and voice notes are kept in private storage that only the two of you can access. We, as the people who run the service, can technically reach what is stored on our servers; we only do so to keep the app running, to look into a report or a problem you ask us about, or when the law requires it.

End-to-end encrypted chat (if you choose). The two of you can turn on end-to-end encryption for your chat from the lock icon in the chat. From then on your messages, and the photos and voice notes you send in the chat, are locked on your phone with keys that only your two phones hold, and are only unlocked on your partner's. Our servers only ever hold the locked copies, so nobody else can read them, us included. We can still see that a message was sent, when and by whom, so that the app can deliver it. When you turn it on, the app also locks the messages you already had, as far as it can. So that your chat can follow you to a new phone, you can make a recovery code: the app then keeps a backup of your keys on our servers that is itself locked with that code, which only you know. Without the code, the backup cannot be opened by us or by anyone else.

Backups. We take a backup of our database every day and keep each one for 7 days, in the UK or the EU, so that we can recover from a fault. Something you delete can therefore remain in a backup for up to 7 days before it is gone for good. Backups are only used to recover from a fault, never to bring deleted content back into the app. The live updates passed between your phones are kept by our database host for no more than 3 days.

This website and cookies

ourtinyplanet.com sets no cookies, has no analytics or adverts, and loads nothing from other companies: even its fonts come from our own site. If you choose a language from the menu, your browser remembers that choice on your device (it is never sent to us). The free tools (distance, halfway point, time zones and countdown) work entirely in your browser: the places and dates you type never leave your device. The only thing the website sends us is the email address you give the waitlist form.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account, with these exceptions:

  • finished games, your shifts in the Job Centre, your pet's care log and the dates you opened the app: 1 year;
  • notifications: 60 days once read, 180 days if never read; the push token of a phone we have not seen for 180 days;
  • notes you scheduled for later: 30 days after they were delivered;
  • crash reports and usage events (only if you turned them on): 12 months;
  • the records the app keeps to run (levels reached, pool games, when the Couple Plan or the Comet packs were shown, started or ended): 13 months; payment records from RevenueCat are kept after that without your account ID;
  • feedback and reports: 2 years, so that we can follow a case up;
  • the website waitlist: your address goes within 30 days of the launch email, or as soon as you unsubscribe.

Your Stardust and Comets ledger (what you earned, spent and bought) is kept for as long as you have an account, because it is the record of your purchases and balances. Your daily steps are kept for as long as you have an account too, so that the two of you can look back on how far you have walked; you can delete them whenever you like.

If you start signing up and never finish, we delete the account: after 30 days if you never confirmed your email address, and after 90 days if you confirmed it but never made a profile.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. When you do, straight away:

  • your account, profile, settings, push tokens, chat keys and key backup, your steps, moods and trackers, and your private content (private journal entries, and the messages, letters, notes, voice notes and photos you added) are deleted, including the photo and audio files themselves;
  • if you share a world with a partner, that world (the land, the home and what is in it, and your partner's own messages and letters) stays with your partner, so that they do not lose it. The couple's Stardust and Comets stay with the world too. If you were the one who subscribed to the Couple Plan, your partner loses the plan; the subscription itself continues at Apple or Google until you cancel it there;
  • feedback you sent us is kept without your account ID, so it can no longer be linked to you;
  • what you deleted can remain in our backups for up to 7 days (see above), and is then gone for good.

If you cannot get into the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account for you within 30 days.

You can also leave a couple without deleting your account (Settings → Account → Leave this couple). You then start afresh on your own and no longer see the shared world; your partner keeps it, including the messages and letters you sent them. Your own Stardust and Comets stay with you.

If a new partner joins later. What the two of you made before a break-up is for the two of you only. A new partner who joins a world sees nothing that was created before the day they joined: not the chat, letters, voice notes or photos. That older shared content stays readable only to the two people who made it, and is deleted 30 days after both of you have left it, or sooner if either of you asks us.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (and, if you live in the European Economic Area, the EU GDPR) you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong (you can change most of it yourself in the app, such as your name, avatar and settings);
  • ask us to delete your information (you can also delete your account yourself in the app);
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your information, including the records we count up to improve the app;
  • ask for your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, contact us at hello@ourtinyplanet.com from the address you signed up with. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We will reply within one month (if a request is complex we may need up to two more months, and will tell you so within the first month). A copy of your information comes as a file you can open on any computer. It includes what you wrote and added yourself; where something you share also belongs to your partner, such as your chat, we take their rights into account too. We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, or, if you live in the European Economic Area, to the data protection authority where you live. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
