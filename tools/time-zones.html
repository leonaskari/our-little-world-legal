      How it works

      Choose both cities and the hours you're each usually up. The two bars show the same day lined up in real time, so a moment sits in the same place on both: the gold part is when you're both awake. Your city's day runs from midnight to midnight on the top bar; theirs is the same stretch of time, in their clock.

      Times come from each city's official time zone rules, including summer time (daylight saving), for the date you choose. Change the date to a few months ahead to see if anything shifts. Below the bars we also tell you the next date your time difference changes.

      Finding a time that works

      • Our best time to call is the hour inside your overlap that suits you both most: we prefer evenings, lunchtimes and early mornings over the middle of a working day.
      • Keep one fixed call. A regular slot that you both protect is easier than finding a new time every day.
      • Watch the clock changes. The UK and Europe change their clocks on different dates from the US, and countries in the southern hemisphere change the other way. For a few weeks a year your gap can grow or shrink by an hour, and a call time that worked can suddenly land in a meeting.
      • No overlap? Plan around it. Leave voice notes for them to wake up to, and save live calls for weekends when one of you can stay up late.

      Questions

      Does this handle daylight saving time?

      Yes. It uses the official time zone rules built into your browser, so summer time is included for the date you pick. Pick a date later in the year to see the gap then.

      What if our waking hours don't overlap at all?

      We tell you so, and show how much earlier or later one of you would need to be up to share at least half an hour.

      Why does the time difference change during the year?

      Countries change their clocks on different dates, and some don't change them at all. The southern hemisphere moves its clocks the opposite way to the northern. So the gap between two cities can change by an hour for part of the year.

      My city isn't on the list. What should I pick?

      Any nearby city in the same time zone works, because only the time zone matters here. For example, choose Manchester for anywhere in Great Britain.
