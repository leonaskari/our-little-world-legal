    How it works

    Choose the day and time you'll be together, and the city where you'll meet, so the time zone is right. The countdown runs in your browser and ticks down every second. If you're in different time zones, it's still the same moment for both of you, and we show what time that's where you are.

    Copy link makes a link with the date, the city and your first names in it, so your partner sees exactly the same countdown. Nothing is stored on our side. Add to calendar downloads a small calendar file (.ics) that opens in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook, with a reminder the day before.

    Making the wait easier

    • Count sleeps, not days. We show how many nights are left as well. It's a smaller number, and it goes down every morning.
    • Give each week something small. A film started at the same moment, a letter, a call with no time limit. The countdown feels shorter when it has stops along the way.
    • Book the next visit before this one ends. The hardest goodbyes are the ones without a date after them.
    • Plan the first hour. Who meets whom, and where. Talking about it is half the fun.

    Not sure what time it will be for them? The time zone planner lines up both your days. Meeting somewhere new? Find your halfway point.

    Questions

    Which time zone does the countdown use?

    The city you choose. The countdown is the same moment everywhere, so if your partner opens the link in another time zone, they count down to the same instant, and we show what time that will be for them.

    Will my partner see the same countdown?

    Yes. Copy link and send it to them. The date, time, city and any first names you added are in the link, so it opens exactly as you set it.

    Is anything saved?

    No. There's no account and nothing is sent to us. Everything is in the link, so keep it (or add the date to your calendar) to come back to it.

    What happens when the countdown reaches zero?

    It says you're together at last. To start the next one, choose a new date and copy a new link.
