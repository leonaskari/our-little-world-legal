Tools for long-distance couples

Small, free tools for the practical side of being apart: the miles, the meeting point, the clocks and the wait.

  • How far apart are we?The distance between your city and theirs, in miles and kilometres, with a rough flight time.
  • Find your halfway pointThe point exactly between you, the cities nearest to it, and the fairest city to meet in.
  • When are we both awake?Your waking hours side by side across time zones, and the best time to call.
  • Countdown until I see youDays, hours, minutes and seconds until you are together, with a link to share.

Made for couples who live apart

Being in a long-distance relationship comes with its own sums. How far is it, really? Where could we meet without one of us doing all the travelling? When are we both awake? How long until the next visit? These four tools answer those questions in a few seconds.

They all run in your browser. There's nothing to sign up for, and the places and dates you choose stay on your device, unless you copy a link to send to your partner. The link holds your choices, so they see exactly what you see.

What each one does

  • How far apart are we? The distance between your two cities as the crow flies, in miles and kilometres, with a rough flight time.
  • Find your halfway point The point exactly between you, the big cities nearest to it, and the fairest city with a busy airport to meet in.
  • When are we both awake? Your waking hours side by side, the best time to call, and what time it is for them, with summer time worked out for you.
  • Countdown until I see you A live countdown to your next visit that you can share, with a calendar file to save the date.

Our Tiny Planet does this every day

The tools above are the small version. In the app, the Lighthouse shows both your clocks and a countdown to your next visit, slow mail takes as long as the real miles to arrive, and your steps carry you along the real route to each other.

Our Tiny Planet is a little shared world for couples, near or far: build a home together, play, write, and keep each other in your day. It's coming soon to iPhone and Android.
