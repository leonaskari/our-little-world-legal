Support

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Need a hand? Email us at hello@ourtinyplanet.com and we will get back to you, usually within a day. You can also write to us from the app: Settings → Legal and support → Contact support, or Report a problem.

Getting started

How do my partner and I pair up?

One of you signs up and gets an invite code. Your partner downloads Our Tiny Planet, signs up, and enters that code to join your world. To see your code again, tap the empty spot on your land where your partner will stand.

Can I start before my partner joins?

Yes. You can pick your land and design your home on your own; when your partner joins, they catch up and you see each other for the first time.

Who can use the app?

Our Tiny Planet is for adults (18 and over). We ask for your date of birth when you sign up.

Purchases and the Couple Plan

What are Stardust and Comets?

Stardust is earned by spending time together in the app: messages, letters, games, check-ins and more. Comets are an optional extra you can buy in the app (Menu → Wallet → Get Comets). Both are used for buildings, upgrades and Store items.

What is the Couple Plan?

One subscription for you both: every upgrade in every building and your house, double Stardust, 50 Comets a week each and a free Store gift each month. Either partner subscribes and both get it. It is £7.99 a month, or £49.99 a year with a 7-day free trial. Find it in Menu → Wallet → Couple Plan, or Settings → Couple Plan.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not in the app. On iPhone: open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Our Tiny Planet. On Android: open Google Play → your profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid the next charge; you keep the plan until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought something but it didn't arrive

Go to Settings → Couple Plan and tap Restore purchases. If it is still missing, email us with roughly when you bought it and what it was, and we will sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid through. For the App Store, visit reportaproblem.apple.com; for Google Play, use the order in your Google Play account. If a purchase is refunded, the Comets it gave are taken back (as many as are still in your wallet).

Your account and privacy

How do I leave my couple?

Settings → Account → Leave this couple. This ends all contact between you straight away. You start afresh on your own and keep your Stardust and Comets; your former partner keeps the world you built and gets a new invite code. If they pair with someone new, that person sees nothing that was created before the day they joined. This cannot be undone.

When you leave, you can choose to Take back my photos and voice notes (on unless you turn it off: everything of this kind you added is deleted for both of you; your words stay), to Leave quietly (your partner gets no notification, though they will see in the app that you have gone) and to Block them (the two of you cannot pair again with any invite code; you can lift this in Settings → Privacy and safety).

How do I delete my Our Tiny Planet account?

Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes, straight away, your profile and everything private to you (your messages, letters and notes, the photos and voice notes you added, including the files themselves, your settings and your sign-in); your partner keeps your shared world. If you signed in with Apple, we also tell Apple to forget the app's sign-in. What you deleted can remain in our backups for up to 7 days, then it is gone for good. This cannot be undone.

Can't get into the app? Email hello@ourtinyplanet.com from the address you signed up with (or tell us that address and how you signed in) and ask us to delete your Our Tiny Planet account. We confirm it is you, then delete your account and the same data as above within 30 days, and reply when it is done. What is kept: crash reports and usage events (only if you turned them on, and usage events are not linked to your account) for up to 12 months; feedback and reports you sent, without your account ID, for up to 2 years; and the shared world, which stays with your partner. Apple or Google keep their own record of what you bought, under their privacy policies. The details are in the Privacy Policy. Deleting your account does not cancel a Couple Plan: cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

To delete only some of your data and keep your account, use Settings → Account → Delete some of your data (your chat, including game chats and Truth or Dare answers; your letters; memories; mood and movement; or your photos and voice notes, which removes every photo and voice note you added anywhere, for both of you, and leaves the words around them). To remove a single chat message for both of you, long-press it and tap Unsend or Delete for both of you.

How do I get a copy of my data, or correct it?

You can change your name, avatar and settings yourself in the app. For a copy of your data straight away, go to Settings → Privacy and safety → Download a copy of my data: you get a file (JSON) of what you have added and your account records, up to 5 times a day. Photos and voice notes are listed by name; email us if you want the files too. For anything else under your data protection rights, or if you prefer, email hello@ourtinyplanet.com from the address you signed up with. We reply within one month.

Who can see what I write?

Only you and your partner. Private journal entries are only visible to you. We never sell your data and there are no ads. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I report a problem or someone's behaviour?

Long-press a message (in the chat or a game’s chat) or a photo your partner sent and tap Report, or go to Settings → Legal and support → Report a problem. A person reads every report, normally within a day. If you ever feel unsafe, leave the couple straight away (see above), and contact local emergency services if you are in danger. There is more in Staying safe below.

Staying safe

Report it in the app. Long-press a message or photo your partner sent and tap Report, or use Settings → Privacy and safety. Pick the reason that fits and add anything you want us to know. A person reads every report, normally within a day. We do not tell your partner who reported them, but as there are only two of you, they may guess. What we can do is set out in our Terms of Use.

Leave, quietly if you need to. Settings → Account → Leave this couple. You can leave quietly, so your partner gets no notification, and block them, so they cannot pair with you again.

Take back what you sent. You can delete any chat message you sent, for both of you, at any time, and when you leave you can take back every photo and voice note you added. We cannot take back a copy your partner has already saved or screenshotted.

If someone shares, or threatens to share, an intimate image of you. Report it to us and to the police. In the UK, the Revenge Porn Helpline (0345 6000 459) can help. StopNCII.org works worldwide: it makes a digital fingerprint (a hash) of your image on your own device, without the image leaving it, so that taking-part sites can block it.

If you are being hurt or controlled. In the UK: the National Domestic Abuse Helpline, 0808 2000 247 (24 hours); the Men’s Advice Line, 0808 801 0327.

If you are struggling. Samaritans, 116 123 (UK and Republic of Ireland, any time).

Elsewhere, call your local emergency number or a local helpline. If you are in danger now, call 999 (UK) or 112 (EU).

Contact for authorities and users (EU Digital Services Act)

For authorities. Our single point of contact for the authorities of EU Member States, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services, under Article 11 of the Digital Services Act, is hello@ourtinyplanet.com. Please write “DSA” in the subject line. You can write to us in English.

For users. Our single point of contact for users, under Article 12, is the same address, hello@ourtinyplanet.com, or in the app: Settings → Legal and support → Report a problem. A person, not an automated system, reads and answers every message. You can write in English or in any language the app is available in; we may use translation tools to read it, and we will reply in English or in your language.

To report illegal content. In the app, use Report a problem, or long-press a message or photo and tap Report. If you do not use the app (for example, if a photo of you was shared without your consent), email hello@ourtinyplanet.com with: why you think the content is illegal; enough detail for us to find it (for example, which account shared it and when); your name and email address (you do not need to give your name if you are reporting child sexual abuse material); and a statement that you believe your report is accurate and complete. We confirm that we have received it, decide on it promptly and tell you what we decided.

In an emergency, if someone’s life or safety is at risk, contact your local emergency services first (999 in the UK, 112 in the EU).

Something not working?

  • Not getting notifications: check Settings → Notifications in the app, and that notifications are allowed for Our Tiny Planet in your phone's settings.
  • Something looks stuck: close the app fully and open it again, and make sure you have the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
  • Still stuck: email us at hello@ourtinyplanet.com with what happened and your phone model, and a screenshot if you can.
