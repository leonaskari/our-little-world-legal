1. Who can use Our Tiny Planet

You must be 18 or over. By using the app, you confirm that you are. You need an account, and you are responsible for keeping your sign-in details safe and for what happens on your account.

2. Your shared world

Our Tiny Planet is shared between you and one partner, who joins with your invite code. Anything you share in the app (for example messages, letters, photos, voice notes and shared journal entries) can be seen by your partner. Only invite someone you trust.

If you delete your account, your private content is deleted, including your photos and voice notes. The world you shared (the land, the home and what is in it) stays with your partner so that they do not lose it. If your partner later pairs with someone new, that person sees nothing that was created before the day they joined.

You can delete any chat message you sent, for both of you, at any time, and when you leave a couple you can take back the photos and voice notes you added (see our Privacy Policy). We cannot take back a copy your partner has already saved or screenshotted.

3. Your content

You own what you create and share in Our Tiny Planet. You give us permission to store it, process it and show it to you and your partner, only so that we can provide the app. This permission ends when the content is deleted.

You must not share anything that is illegal, that you do not have the right to share, or that harasses, threatens or harms another person.

If someone makes you feel unsafe, you can leave the couple straight away (Settings → Account → Leave this couple), quietly and with a block if you want, and report it to us (long-press a message or photo and tap Report, or Settings → Legal and support → Report a problem). A person reads every report, normally within a day, and we may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms (see section 5).

5. Safety, illegal content and what we may restrict

What is not allowed. You must not use Our Tiny Planet to share or store anything illegal. This includes, in particular: child sexual abuse material or any sexual content involving anyone under 18 (we report it to the UK National Crime Agency); intimate images of anyone without their consent, or threats to share them; content that threatens, harasses, stalks, controls or coerces another person; terrorist content; extreme pornography; fraud and scams; and content that encourages suicide or serious self-harm. You must also not share anything that breaks section 3 of these terms. Sexually explicit or pornographic content is not allowed either, including in private messages to your partner.

How we find out. We do not scan your private content. Most of it can only be seen by you and your partner, and a chat you choose to end-to-end encrypt cannot be read by us. We act when someone reports content to us, when an authority tells us about it, or when we otherwise become aware of it. We do not use automated tools to detect content or to decide what to do about it.

How to report. In the app, long-press a message (in the chat or a game’s chat) or a photo your partner sent and tap Report, or go to Settings → Privacy and safety or Settings → Legal and support → Report a problem. When you report a message, your phone sends us its words, so that we can read it even in an encrypted chat. Anyone, including people who do not use the app, can email hello@ourtinyplanet.com. If someone is in danger, contact the emergency services first.

What we do, and how fast. A person reads every report, normally within a day. If content is illegal or breaks these terms, we remove it as quickly as we can: for child sexual abuse, terrorist content and intimate images shared without consent, as soon as we have confirmed it, and within 48 hours of a valid report where we can. We may also suspend or close the account that shared it, and we may end the couple. We keep the evidence the law requires, and we report to the UK National Crime Agency, the police or other authorities where the law requires it (such as for child sexual abuse) or where someone’s life or safety is at risk. We do not tell the person reported who made the report.

We tell you why. If we remove your content or restrict your account, we tell you what we did, why (the law or the part of these terms involved), whether it came from a report, and how to challenge it, unless telling you would put someone at risk or harm a police investigation.

Complaints and appeals. If you think we got a decision wrong, or that we are not meeting our safety or privacy duties, reply to our message or email hello@ourtinyplanet.com with “Appeal” in the subject. We will look at the decision again with fresh eyes, normally within 14 days, and put things right if we were wrong. You can also go to court, and if you live in the European Union you can contact your national Digital Services Coordinator. Our contact details for authorities and users under the EU Digital Services Act are in our Privacy Policy.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their rules and your legal rights; if a purchase is refunded, we take back the Comets it gave, as many as are still in your wallet. Your Stardust and Comets belong to your account. They do not expire while you have an account, and they cannot be transferred, except that you can send Stardust to your partner in the app. If you leave a couple, you keep your own. If you delete your account, your Stardust and Comets are deleted with it and are not refunded (this does not affect your legal rights). If we ever stop providing Our Tiny Planet, we will tell you in the app at least 60 days before, and we will explain how to ask for a refund of Comets bought in the 90 days before that notice and not yet spent.

The Store’s mystery box (one a day, paid for with Stardust) gives you a random item you do not have yet. The chance of each kind of item is shown in the Store before you open it. If there is nothing left for you to get, you receive 30 Stardust instead.

The Couple Plan. The Couple Plan is an optional subscription, monthly or yearly, bought through the App Store or Google Play by either partner (you must be 18 or over). While it is active, every building and house upgrade is open for both partners in the couple, Stardust from everyday things counts double, each partner receives 50 Comets a week, and each partner can pick one Store item a month for free (seasonal items are not included). It does not include buildings themselves or land upgrades. The price and length of each plan are shown in the app before you buy, as the store sets them for your country. The yearly plan may start with a free trial; if you do not cancel before the trial ends, the subscription begins and you are charged. Two days before a free trial ends, we remind you in the app (and by notification, if you allow them) that it will renew unless you cancel. Payment is charged to your App Store or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase (or when the free trial ends). The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before it ends. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings; cancelling stops the next renewal and the plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Any unused part of a free trial is lost when you subscribe. When the plan ends, upgrades you did not buy lock again; Comets already given stay in your wallet, and nothing you made with the upgrades is deleted. If you leave the couple, the plan stays with the store account that bought it.

Your right to cancel. Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play, and the store's terms explain how to cancel a purchase or ask for a refund. If you live in the UK or the EU, you normally have 14 days to cancel a purchase of digital content, but that right ends once the content has been supplied with your agreement. Comets, bundles and the Couple Plan's benefits are supplied as soon as the purchase goes through: by buying, you ask for them straight away and accept that the right to cancel then ends. This does not stop you cancelling the Couple Plan at any time so that it does not renew, and it does not affect a refund the store gives under its own policies, or your legal rights if something you bought is faulty or not as described.
