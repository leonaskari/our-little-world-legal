A day on your tiny planet

However many miles and hours apart you are, your planet keeps you in each other's day.

  1. 08:00☀️

    Good morning

    You wake to their good night, and today's question waiting for you both. Answer it, and see what they said.

  2. 12:30💌

    A letter arrives

    They wrote it yesterday. Letters take a night to travel, the way the best ones always have.

  3. 18:00🎲

    Your move

    Their turn of Ludo came in while you were at work. Play yours on the bus home; they'll see it when they wake.

  4. 20:00👣

    A little closer

    Your steps from Apple Health carry you another few kilometres across the map towards their city.

  5. 22:30🌙

    Good night

    Feed the pet, send a heartbeat, leave a note on the fridge. The planet goes quiet, and keeps your place till tomorrow.

Take a look inside

Everything on your planet is shared. When one of you builds, decorates or tidies, the other sees it too.

Your land

Build your planet, plot by plot

Choose a land (a forest valley, a lantern lake, snowy peaks…) and raise your buildings along the river, one at a time. Animals wander, the weather changes, day turns to night, and there's always something new to find.

Your home

A home that's yours, together

Design the outside, then decorate every room: furniture, wallpaper, the garden. Sit on the sofa together, leave a surprise for them to find, and see when they were last home.

You, but anime

Be exactly who you are

Make your character down to the hair, eyes and outfit, then keep dressing up from the Store: matching looks, glowing auras, little crowns.

The Arcade

Games that work across time zones

Chess, Ludo, a word game with a real dictionary, pool, brain battles and more. Take turns whenever suits you both, or play live when you're both there.

The Mailroom

Letters that arrive tomorrow

Write a proper letter and it lands the next day, sealed with a stamp. Add voice notes, photo notes and letters to open when… you miss them, can't sleep, or need a smile.

The Pet Sanctuary

A pet you raise together

Adopt, feed, play and groom it between you. Watch it grow, teach it tricks and send it on little adventures.

The Observatory

Dream about what's next

A shared vision board, wishes on a star, trips you're planning, a map of the places you've been together, and savings towards the next time you meet.

The Big Mess

Three thousand books. One very messy library.

Step inside a magical 3D library and put every book back on its shelf, in order. Out of the windows, a whole universe drifts by: moons, nebulae, a ringed planet and shooting stars.

Together

See each other, even miles apart

When you're both in the library you walk round it together, each as the character you chose. In the house you see who was there last. On the land you can send a mood with a single tap.

Walk to each other

Every step brings you closer

Connect Apple Health and your steps carry you along the real route to their city, on a map, with both of you walking. London to New York is about 7.4 million steps. You'll get there.

Seven lands. Pick yours.

Each has its own sky, animals, trees and weather. Wherever you settle, every building fits.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You can build your home, raise every building and play every day without paying. Extras (upgrades, Store items, Comets and the Couple Plan) are optional.

Do we both need the app?

Yes: it's a planet for two. Download it, make your character, then share your invite code so your partner can join your planet. Our Tiny Planet is on iPhone.

We're in different time zones. Does it still work?

That's what it's made for. Games are turn by turn, letters arrive the next day, and the Lighthouse shows you both clocks and when it's a good time to call.

Can we play at the same time?

Yes. Walk round the library together, play live games, sit together at home, and see each other move on the land.

Is what we share private?

What you make together is only for the two of you. Your steps are read from Apple Health only if you allow it, and never used for advertising. Read the privacy policy.

Who is it for?

Couples aged 18 and over, especially those living apart, for a semester, a posting, or across the world.
